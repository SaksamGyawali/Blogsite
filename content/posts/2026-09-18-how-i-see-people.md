title: "How I see people?"
date: 2026-09-18
tags: [essay]
description: "Seen objectively, every person is an experiment the universe is running — and every experience gets committed to a repository nobody can opt out of."
---

When I see people subjectively, I pour in my biases, frustrations, opinions,
prejudices, jealousy, and so on. I am not talking about that today.

When I see people objectively, I see them as the experiments of the universe,
where everyone is playing their role.

I believe there is a large corpus of knowledge in the universe — a repository.
We are committing, staging and merging our experiments, which are our
experiences. Unlike the real computer world, everything is open source here.
You are contributing whether you want to or not.

So each human experience is knowledge, for the future or for the present. Even
if a person does not achieve anything by current standards of success, he is
contributing that knowledge. If a person is regretful about his life on his
deathbed, he is contributing that knowledge.

So no human, and no life, is useless to the universe. You can feel useless to
yourself; you are still contributing information to the world.

You might be useful as either:

1. Following your path leads to guilt, regret, or being hung till death.
2. Following your path leads to success and happiness by current standards.

Ultimately, everyone is contributing to the open source whether they want to or
not.

That leaves the question: so, should everyone follow what they want?

Aren't they already following what they want?

1. Choosing to be how they are is their own choice.
2. Choosing to change is their own choice.

The better question to ask would be: given that I am making choices, what kind
of experiment would my life be?
